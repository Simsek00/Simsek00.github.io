4.2 Data Google processes to serve adverts

The game is free and is paid for by adverts, served by Google AdMob — operated by Google Ireland Limited in the European Economic Area and by the relevant Google entity elsewhere.

To decide which advert to show, the Google advertising SDK typically receives the following from your device:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) — a resettable device-level advertising identifier
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it (usually country or city level; this is not GPS location)
  • Device and software information — device model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type
  • Advert interaction — whether an advert was shown, how long it was viewed, whether it was tapped
  • App information — the application identifier and version
  • Anti-fraud signals — technical signals used to detect invalid traffic and fraudulent clicks

This information goes directly to Google. Meok Apps does not receive it, cannot see it and does not store it. How Google uses it is described in Google advertising technologies and privacy and the Google Privacy Policy.

All we see in the AdMob console are aggregate reports that identify nobody: total impressions, click-through rate, a country breakdown and estimated revenue. These are not about individual users and do not allow us to identify you.

4.3 What is never processed

Neither the game nor we ever request, collect or store any of the following:

  • Name, e-mail address, telephone number or postal address
  • Identity, passport or tax numbers or other official identifiers
  • Card, bank or payment details
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts, call logs, SMS, photos, gallery or files
  • Microphone or camera recordings
  • Health, biometric or other special-category data
  • Social media accounts, browsing history or installed-app lists
  • Any profile built about you

The game requests no camera, microphone, location, contacts, file or notification permission. The only system permissions it uses are the internet access and advertising-identifier access required by the Google advertising SDK.

6. Advertising in detail

There are two kinds of advert:

1. Interstitial (full-screen). Shown only after a match has finished, at a natural break. Its frequency is capped: at most one every three matches, and only if at least two minutes have passed since the previous one. There is never an advert during a match simulation, while navigating menus, or on app start.

2. Rewarded (optional). Played only when you choose to watch one, in exchange for an in-game benefit — for example revealing a player's potential, completing a pending scout report immediately, or playing three weeks at once. Declining costs you nothing: an entire career can be played without watching a single advert.

There is no paid ad-free version. If your device is offline, no adverts are shown and the game continues to work in full.

12. Device backup and device transfer

If you have Android's backup or new-device transfer feature switched on, your game saves and settings may be copied by Android to your Google account, alongside those of your other apps. The app enables this deliberately so that you do not lose your careers when you change device.

This is a feature of your device and Google account and is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Meok Apps has no access to those backups. You can turn it off in your device settings (Settings → Google → Backup).

13. Security

  • Your game data sits in an app-private area protected by the operating system; under normal conditions other apps cannot read it. Rooting or jailbreaking the device weakens that protection.
  • We run no server holding your personal data, so there is no server-side breach risk.
  • Network traffic created by the Google advertising SDK is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • The app is published through Google Play's signature verification. Download the game only from the official store; modified copies are outside the scope of this policy.

No method is perfectly secure, but keeping the data-processing surface as small as possible is a deliberate design choice.

14. Children's privacy

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. It is not published under the Google Play Families programme. It has no chat, no user-generated content, no social features and no purchases.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child's data has been processed can write to 55mehmetokur@gmail.com; if any such data has reached us, we will delete it without delay.

  6. Silinmesini veya yok edilmesini isteme
